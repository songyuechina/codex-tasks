About Switching to the Model Space Layout (VBA/ActiveX)

 
 
 

From paper space, you can switch to model space floating viewports or model space tiled viewports.

Topics in this section

  • To Switch to Floating Viewports (VBA/ActiveX)

    You can switch from paper space to model space in a floating viewport on a layout.

  • To Switch to Tiled Viewports (VBA/ActiveX)

    Model space can be divided into multiple tiled viewports.
