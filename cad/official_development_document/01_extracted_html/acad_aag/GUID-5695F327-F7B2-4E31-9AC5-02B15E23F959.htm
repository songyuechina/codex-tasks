About Updating Xrefs (VBA/ActiveX)

 
 
 

When you open or plot your drawing, AutoCAD reloads each xref to reflect the latest state of the referenced drawing.

After you make changes to an externally referenced drawing and save the file, other users can access your changes immediately by reloading the xref.
