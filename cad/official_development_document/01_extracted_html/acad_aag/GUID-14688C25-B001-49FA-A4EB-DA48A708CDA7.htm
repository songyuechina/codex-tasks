About Working with Layers (VBA/ActiveX)

 
 
 

You are always drawing on a layer. It may be the default layer or a layer you create and name yourself.

Each layer has an associated color and linetype. For example, you can create a layer on which you draw only centerlines and assign the color blue and the linetype CENTER to that layer. Then, whenever you want to draw centerlines you can switch to that layer and start drawing.

All layers and linetypes are kept within their parent Collection objects. Layers are kept within the Layers collection, and linetypes are kept within the Linetypes collection.

Topics in this section

  • About Sorting Layers and Linetypes (VBA/ActiveX)

    You can iterate through the Layers and Linetypes collections to find all the layers and linetypes in a drawing.

  • About Creating and Naming Layers (VBA/ActiveX)

  • About Making a Layer Active (VBA/ActiveX)

    You are always drawing on the active layer.

  • About Turning Layers on and off (VBA/ActiveX)

    Turned-off layers are regenerated with the drawing but are not displayed or plotted.

  • About Freezing and Thawing Layers (VBA/ActiveX)

    You can freeze layers to speed up display changes, improve object selection performance, and reduce regeneration time for complex drawings.

  • About Locking and Unlocking Layers (VBA/ActiveX)

    You cannot edit the objects on a locked layer; however, they are still visible if the layer is on and thawed.

  • About Assigning Color to a Layer (VBA/ActiveX)

    You can assign color to a layer. Colors are identified by the AcCmColor object. This object can hold an RGB value, an ACI number (an integer from 1 to 255), or a named color.

  • About Assigning a Linetype to a Layer (VBA/ActiveX)

    When you are defining layers, linetypes provide another way to convey visual information.

  • About Deleting Layers (VBA/ActiveX)

    You can delete a layer at any time during a drawing session.
