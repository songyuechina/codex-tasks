About Using Zoom (VBA/ActiveX)

 
 
 

A view is a specific magnification, position, and orientation of a drawing.

The most common way to change a view is to use one of the many AutoCAD Zoom options, which increases or decreases the size of the image displayed in the graphics area.

Topics in this section

  • About Defining a Zoom Window (VBA/ActiveX)

    You can quickly zoom in on an area by specifying the corners that define it.

  • About Scaling a View (VBA/ActiveX)

    You can increase or decrease the magnification of the image by a precise scale.

  • About Centering Objects (VBA/ActiveX)

    Zoom in on the active drawing to a specified center.

  • About Displaying Drawing Limits and Extents (VBA/ActiveX)

    You can display a view based on the drawing boundaries or the extents of the objects in the drawing with the ZoomAll, ZoomExtents, or ZoomPrevious method.
