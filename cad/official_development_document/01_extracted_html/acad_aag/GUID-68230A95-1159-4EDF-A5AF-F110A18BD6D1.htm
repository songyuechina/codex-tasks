About Clipping Images (VBA/ActiveX)

 
 
 

You can define a region of an image for display and plotting by clipping the image.

The clipping boundary must be a 2D polygon or rectangle with vertices constrained to lie within the boundaries of the image. Multiple instances of the same image can have different boundaries.
