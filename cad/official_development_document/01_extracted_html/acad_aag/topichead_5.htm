Dimensions, Leaders, and Geometric Tolerances

 
 
 

Topics in this section

  • About Dimensioning Concepts (VBA/ActiveX)

    Dimensions show the geometric measurements of objects, the distances or angles between objects, or the X and Y coordinates of a feature.

  • About Creating Dimensions (VBA/ActiveX)

    You can create linear, radial, angular, and ordinate dimensions.

  • About Editing Dimensions (VBA/ActiveX)

    As with other graphical objects in AutoCAD, you can edit dimensions using the standard methods and properties provided for the object.

  • About Working with Dimension Styles (VBA/ActiveX)

    A named dimension style is a group of settings that determines the appearance of the dimension. Using named dimension styles, you can establish and enforce drafting standards for drawings.

  • About Dimensioning in Model Space and Paper Space (VBA/activeX)

    You can draw dimensions in both paper space and model space.

  • About Creating Leaders and Annotation (VBA/ActiveX)

    A leader is a line that connects some annotation to a feature in a drawing.

  • About Using Geometric Tolerances (VBA/ActiveX)

    Geometric tolerancing shows deviations of form, profile, orientation, location, and runout of a feature.
