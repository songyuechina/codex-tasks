About Deleting Linetypes (VBA/ActiveX)

 
 
 

To delete a linetype, use the Delete method.

You can delete a linetype at any time during a drawing session; however, linetypes that cannot be deleted include BYLAYER, BYBLOCK, CONTINUOUS, the current linetype, and xref-dependent linetypes. Also, linetypes referenced by block definitions cannot be deleted, even if they are not used by any objects.
