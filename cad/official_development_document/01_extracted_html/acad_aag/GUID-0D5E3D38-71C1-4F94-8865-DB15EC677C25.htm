About Creating Rays (VBA/ActiveX)

 
 
 

A ray is a line in 3D space that starts at a point you specify and extends to infinity.

Unlike xline construction lines, which extend in two directions, rays extend in only one direction. As a result, rays help reduce the visual clutter caused by numerous construction lines.

Like construction lines, rays are ignored by commands that display the drawing extents.
